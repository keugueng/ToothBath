Our Services

Scaling & Gingival Care

Professional removal of soft and hard deposits, gingival curettage, and personalized periodontal care.

Dental Examinations & Assessments

Comprehensive oral, periodontal, and extraoral examinations, including oral cancer screening and periodontal measurements.

Preventive Dental Treatments

Selective coronal polishing, tooth desensitization, and fluoride applications to support oral health.

Oral Health Education

Personalized oral hygiene instructions and practical recommendations to help patients maintain healthy smiles.

Nutritional & Lifestyle Counselling

Guidance on nutrition and tobacco cessation to promote healthier habits and better oral health.

Dental Impressions & Denture Care

Alginate dental impressions, denture cleaning, care, and labelling services.

Residential Care Education

Customized oral health education sessions for residential care facility staff and patients' families.

Professional Referrals & Collaboration

Coordination with general dentists and dental specialists, including oral surgeons, endodontists, periodontists, and dental prosthetists.
